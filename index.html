Mindfulness, minus the myths

You don't need to close your eyes, sit cross-legged, or find perfect silence. Moments gently reminds you that being present fits into real life:

  • Keep your eyes open — a soft gaze works fine
  • Any posture counts — sit, stand, walk, or lie down
  • Even one mindful minute is real practice
  • Your mind will wander; noticing and returning is the practice

What you can do

🌿

Log a moment

Date, duration, and how you felt — plus an optional activity and a note. It takes seconds.

✨

See your progress

Total mindful hours, your current day streak, and minutes logged this week.

🎯

Spot your patterns

A 7-day chart and a breakdown of how you've been feeling across your practice.

🙏

Get gentle guidance

Bite-size tips for being mindful anywhere, and a daily prompt for when you're not sure how to begin.

Private by design

No sign-up. No login. No cloud. No analytics. No ads.

Everything you log is stored only on your device and never leaves it. Moments makes no network requests at all. If you choose to turn on Apple Health sync, mindful minutes are written to your own Health app on the same device — nothing is sent anywhere else.
